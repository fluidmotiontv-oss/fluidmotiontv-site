About the Lab

The Current

FluidMotion TV is a one-lab network built on a simple obsession: harmonic liquid dynamics — the point where mathematics, sound, and motion become the same thing. Every visual you see here is generated live from that math, tuned to the 432 Hz Pythagorean frequency stack.

The lab ships two kinds of work: interactive WebGL instruments (the demos and portals you're looking at) and the FlowTouch Archive — e-books and software that document the methods behind them.

The Network

FluidMotion runs parallel with Dragon 9, the network's entertainment arm. FluidMotion builds the instruments and the archive; Dragon 9 carries the media, the streams, and the noise. The switcher in the top bar jumps between the two frequencies.

Both properties are progressive web apps — installable on desktop and mobile, working offline after first load, no app store in between.

Install as an App

On desktop, look for the install icon in your browser's address bar. On mobile, use "Add to Home Screen" from the browser menu. The full site — including the demo gallery — works offline once installed.
